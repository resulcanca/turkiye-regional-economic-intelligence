# Methodology & Data Provenance

## Core metric
Mean annual equivalised household disposable income (TL), SR/NUTS Level 2.

## 2025
Income reference year is the previous calendar year according to TÜİK's 2025 Income
Distribution Statistics. The national mean is 332,882 TL.

## Derived metrics
- Income growth = (2025 income / 2024 income - 1) × 100
- Relative income = 2025 regional income / Türkiye 2025 mean
- Ankara gap = Ankara 2025 income - regional 2025 income
- Rank = descending regional income

## Housing snapshot
TCMB January 2026 KFE and New Tenant Rent Index values are included only for Türkiye,
İstanbul, Ankara and İzmir because those exact values are directly documented in the
published TCMB January 2026 table.

## Important limitation
Do not interpret the composite "pressure proxy" as an official economic index. It is a
portfolio-analysis construct with transparent weights. The project intentionally separates
official indicators from analyst-derived scores.
